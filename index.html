Privacy & Support

One page, because there is very little to say: the game keeps everything on your device.

Short version. No account. No cloud. No ad SDK. No analytics service. Credits, streak and solved boards are stored in the app's local storage on your iPhone. Nothing is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Privacy policy

What we collect

Nothing. Crowns does not have a server, a login, or a third-party SDK. The app does not request your name, email, contacts, location, photos or health data.

What is stored on your device

  • credit balance, daily streak, solved day numbers, unlocked archive days
  • your settings (reminder time, haptics)
  • on-device usage counters (opens, solves, store views) used only to show you nothing — they never leave the device

Deleting the app deletes this data. There is no backup on our side, because we never receive it.

Purchases

Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us only aggregate sales reports. Consumable credits cannot be restored because they are spent in the game.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, iOS schedules a local notification on your device at the hour you chose. No push server is involved. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Support

Common questions

  • I lost my streak. Streaks follow the puzzle day, which changes at local midnight. Solving within the day keeps it.
  • Credits did not arrive. Open the credit screen once with a connection; purchases are verified with Apple at that moment. If it still fails, use “Restore” — consumables cannot be restored, so contact us with the Apple receipt.
  • The daily board is too hard. Use a hint (1 credit) or Zen mode for unlimited practice boards.
  • Can I play offline? Yes. Boards are generated on the device.
  • Which languages? English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish (ES/MX), Italian, Portuguese (BR), Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

Contact

Email support@serdarozdemir.dev. Please include your iOS version and the app version from Settings. We answer within two business days.
